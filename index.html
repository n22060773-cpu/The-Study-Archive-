Welcome To The Study Archive

Welcome to The Study Archive, a collection of study resources created to help Year 10 students feel more prepared, supported and confident during their final candidate year. By bringing useful resources and student guidance together, the website aims to make students feel seen, valued and supported.

The website brings together useful resources, study methods and guidance in one organised place, making it easier to find what you need when preparing for assessments.

What Is The Study Archive?

The Study Archive is a place where Year 10 students can find organised academic resources and study guidance. It includes subjects such as Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, Kiswahili, French and Humanities.
